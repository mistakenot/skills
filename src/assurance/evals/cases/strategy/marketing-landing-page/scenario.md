We're launching a new product next month and I need a landing page for the
marketing site. It's the first thing prospects see, so it has to look great and
convert — this is going straight onto our main marketing domain and the whole
company will be sharing the link.

The page:
- A hero section with a headline, subhead, product screenshot, and a primary
  "Request a demo" call-to-action.
- Three feature highlight cards.
- A short "logos of companies using us" strip.
- A footer with links to docs, pricing, and social.
- The demo CTA opens a short form (name + work email) that posts the lead to our
  CRM webhook.

Marketing owns the copy and will be iterating on the headline, imagery, and
feature blurbs constantly — probably several times a week as we A/B different
messaging in the run-up to launch. The page is a static Next.js build deployed
to our CDN.

Before writing any code, design a solid testing strategy for this project. Then
we'll implement the page and its tests according to that strategy.
